**Nuts**: ISO 4032 (regular), ISO 4035 (thin), ISO 10511 (nylon insert lock nut), DIN 562 (square, thin), ISO 4161 (flange),
DIN 1587 (acorn: the screw length stops inside its dome).
**Washers**: ISO 7089 (plain), ISO 7093 (large), DIN 127 (split lock), under the nut and under the head. A nut or washer that does not exist in a size (thin nut M2, large washer M2…)
is replaced by the plain one, with a message.

**Drives** (cap, countersunk, button, set screws): hexagon socket, Torx (ISO 14579, 14581, 14583), Phillips, Pozidriv, slotted
(ISO 2009, ISO 1580…). The designation follows the drive.

**Grade**: 8.8, 10.9, 12.9, stainless A2-70 and A4-70 — written in the bill of materials with the matching nut class and washer, and
used for the look (zinc-plated steel, black, stainless; brass inserts, ground pins) when *Materials* is on.

**Thread**: *Simplified* (smooth, the thread end marked by a small step: light, drawn as ISO 6410 asks by Ichno) or *Modeled*
(real ISO metric helix, for renders).

Shoulder screw (ISO 7379) dimensions come from suppliers' catalogues: check them against yours.
