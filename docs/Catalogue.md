**Nuts**: ISO 4032 (regular), ISO 4035 (thin), ISO 10511 (nylon insert lock nut), DIN 562 (square, thin — the usual captive nut in 3D
printing), ISO 4161 (flange), DIN 1587 (acorn: the screw length stops inside its dome), T-nuts for 20 / 30 / 40 aluminium profiles (slot
6 / 8 / 10: the nut sits under the slot's lip, nothing is drilled in the profile), ASME B18.2.2 hex nuts for inch screws.
**Washers**: ISO 7089 (plain), ISO 7093 (large), DIN 127 (split lock), SAE (inch), under the nut and under the head. A nut or washer that does not exist in a size (thin nut M2, large washer M2…)
is replaced by the plain one, with a message.

**Drives** (cap, countersunk, button, set screws): hexagon socket, Torx (ISO 14579, 14581, 14583), Phillips, Pozidriv, slotted
(ISO 2009, ISO 1580…). The designation follows the drive.

**Grade**: 8.8, 10.9, 12.9, stainless A2-70 and A4-70 — written in the bill of materials with the matching nut class and washer, and
used for the look (zinc-plated steel, black, stainless; brass inserts, ground pins) when *Materials* is on.

**Thread**: *Simplified* (smooth, the thread end marked by a small step: light, drawn as ISO 6410 asks by Ichno) or *Modeled*
(real ISO metric helix, for renders).

**Wood screws** (DIN 7505 type, countersunk, Pozidriv): clearance in the first board, pilot hole in the next one — 0.55 d in softwood,
0.7 d in hardwood, 0.5 d in chipboard / MDF (setting *Wood*); Auto Length gives 5 d of engagement without going through.

**Inch** (UNC): socket head cap screws ASME B18.3 #4-40 to 1/2-13, with inch lengths (1/4" to 6"), clearance holes (close, normal,
loose), counterbores and tap drills; a metric nut or washer chosen in the panel becomes the inch one by itself.

Shoulder screw (ISO 7379), wood screw and T-nut dimensions come from suppliers' catalogues: check them against yours.

Not yet: UNF, other inch heads, wing nuts.
