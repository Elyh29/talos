# Editing

- **Edit a placed screw**: in *Place by Clicking*, click a screw (head, nut, washer or insert), or select it and press **Edit Screw**.
  Its settings come back in the panel and the preview is pinned on it; the real screw is hidden. Change it, or click elsewhere to
  move it. **Enter** replaces it (old holes removed, new ones cut), **Esc** gives it back as it was.
- **Move with G** (or turn with R): the holes follow while you drag, then are made again where the screw stops — thicknesses and parts
  measured again (*Remake Holes When Moved*, on by default).
- **Shift+D, Ctrl+M**: copies get their own holes; a mirrored screw is rebuilt without a negative scale. A screw deleted with **X**
  leaves nothing behind (its cutters, nut and washers go with it).
- **Update Selected** rebuilds the selected screws with the panel's settings; in its operator panel (F9) untick what each screw keeps of
  its own — only *Size*, for instance, changes the size of screws of different types. The eyedropper copies a screw's settings to the panel.
- **Remove Screws** deletes the selected screws with their holes, nuts and washers.
- A part copied with Shift+D keeps its own holes (its Boolean no longer points to the original's).
