# Changelog

## 1.0.3
- Patterns in Talos: screws near the corners, in a line or on a circle (Tab, Page Up/Down, R), also on curved parts

## 1.0.2
- Preview label: the same hole through several parts is written once, with the number of parts

## 1.0.1
- The whole catalogue in Talos: wood screws, inch screws (UNC), T-nuts, inch hex nuts, SAE washers
- Through bolts always get their nut outside the part
- Screws placed with another edition: the eyedropper skips settings not available here, editing them is refused with a message

## 1.0.0 — first public release
- Place by Clicking: a ghost of the real screw with its holes, a label and a section card; keys, magnet guides, grid; Alt+Shift+T, Shift+A
- Holes: ISO 273 clearance, DIN 974-1 counterbore, 90° countersink, ISO 2306 tapped hole, H7 pin hole; auto length; four joints
- Catalogue: 13 standards from M2 to M20, nuts, washers, drives, grades and materials
- Edit a placed screw; Shift+D and Ctrl+M copies keep their holes; holes made again after a move; everything in one Talos collection
- Each part described for Ichno: clean drawings, ISO sections, hole callouts, bill of materials
- English, French, German and Spanish; Blender 4.3 to 5.3
